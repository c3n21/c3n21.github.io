1. **No Unverified Metrics:**

   - Replace generic fluff (e.g., "results-driven problem solver") with verifiable technical facts and concrete deliverables.

2. **Education Clarity:**
   - Education entry must be unambiguous: `BSc Computer Science — ongoing, part-time` at the University of Milan.
   - Never imply or state that the degree was completed in 2019 or is full-time.

3. **Experience Separation:**
   - Homelab, personal infrastructure, and personal OSS projects must be accurately described and never conflated with employer-sponsored production experience.
   - Production enterprise experience (React, TypeScript, .NET microfrontends, middleware, APIs) must remain visible as evidence of production breadth.

4. **Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure:**
   - Do not disclose non-public client/employer details, internal system architectures, secrets, proprietary source code, internal URLs, customer data, or private incident postmortems.
   - Generalize internal architectures and sensitive implementation details when discussing professional client work.
